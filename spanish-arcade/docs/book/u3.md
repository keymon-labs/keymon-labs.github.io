# Unit 3 working notes (paraphrased)

Senses, body, health and feelings, paper sessions 1, 3, 5, 7, plus the end-of-unit recap. Own words only: story beats are short and generic; names of new characters are for us to invent. Archetype ids: [ARCHETYPES.md](../ARCHETYPES.md). Unit sketches: [UNITS.md](../UNITS.md). Same format as [u1.md](u1.md).

## S1 Playing with the senses
- **Story beats.** No comic. A warm-up word list, then a chat where friends say how they feel today (happy, bored, angry…). A feelings song is not reproduced; write our own.
- **Exercise kinds.** Own word list (`free-list`) · odd one out in clouds of sense verbs and body parts (`odd-one-out`) · listen and match sense to organ (`listen-pick-word`, `match-pairs`) · complete sentences by unscrambling letters: which sense does each friend use (`spell-tiles`) · read the feelings dialogue aloud (`dialogue-complete`, `say-it`) · fill sentences with feeling words from a song (`fill-gap-choice`) · label a body picture with 15 words (`match-pairs` on a body scene) · describe hair and eyes with *ser* and *tener* (`fill-gap-choice`, `type-the-answer`) · guess-who by yes/no questions (`who-is-who`) · mood survey (`survey-tally`) · exit riddle about a taste (`veo-veo`).
- **Language.** Five senses and their verbs; about 15 body parts; about eight feelings (*estar* + adjective, *tener miedo*); hair colours and styles; eye colours and sizes; *ser* for hair type, *tener* for eyes and hair; degree words *muy, bastante, un poco*.
- **Ideas.** Body-part labelling drag game; character creator (choose hair and eyes, hear the description); mystery box of senses.

## S3 What a strange pain
- **Story hooks.** A comic where a friend feels a pain and others suggest remedies, ending with a small surprise about what hurts. Carries body parts, health words and advice.
- **Exercise kinds.** Read comic, act out (off-app) · comprehension with three choices and one true/false (`read-answer`) · draw who helps when ill (off-app) · listen and number pictures for advice (`listen-pick-word`) · write advice with *puedes, ¿por qué no…?, tienes que, te aconsejo* (`dialogue-complete`, `fill-gap-choice`) · reflexive verbs: choose me / te / se (`fill-gap-choice`, `verb-conjugator`) · the s / x contrast (`spell-fill-letters` focus s/x) · order syllables into words (`syllable-order`) · crossword (`crossword-lite`) · read aloud advice (`say-it`).
- **Language.** *Me duele(n)* + body part; five advice patterns; reflexive daily-routine verbs with me / te / se; the letters s and x: *x* in words like xilófono, examen, taxi, extraño; *s* in sonrisa, sueño, serpiente.
- **Ideas.** "Doctor's round" game: match a symptom to advice; reflexive routine sequence puzzle; s / x sorting race.

## S5 Yesterday in the park
- **Story hooks.** A story where friends use each sense in a park to find where a strong smell comes from. Models sense verbs, nature words and a sequence of events.
- **Exercise kinds.** Put story pictures in order (`order-words` on events, `read-answer`) · match scene items to senses (`sort-into-bins`) · draw yourself in the park (off-app) · retell in four steps with first, then, later, finally (`order-words`) · the *veo veo* guessing game (`veo-veo`) · conjugate normal and reflexive verbs: yo / tú / él-ella (`verb-conjugator`) · choose me / te / se (`fill-gap-choice`) · reorder jumbled sentences (`order-words`) · listen and circle clothes, times and places (`listen-pick-word`) · hide-and-seek on a grid game (`bingo`-like grid, `place-the-object`) · split a run-on sentence (`spot-the-words`) · word search with exit letters (`word-search`).
- **Language.** About 10 park and nature words; clothes; five sequence words; reflexive verbs with a table: wash, comb, put on, look at, take off; times of day.
- **Ideas.** Smell mystery detective game; routine sequence cards; grid battleship in the park with letters and numbers.

## S7 I feel super
- **Story beats.** No comic. A short text describing a friend who gets special powers (what she can hear and smell, how she looks), vocabulary matching, then inventing a superhero and superpowers.
- **Exercise kinds.** Read a description, match phrases (`match-pairs`, `read-answer`) · answer two questions (`type-the-answer`) · describe your own hero with *es, tiene, está* (`form-card`, `type-the-answer`) · label body parts in a picture (`match-pairs`) · listen and match emotion phrases (`listen-pick-word`) · colour an emotion picture by a colour key (`colour-by-code`) · colour the reflexive verbs in a cloud (`sort-into-bins`) · body-part bingo (`bingo`) · talk about a picture (`say-it`). Craft is off-app.
- **Language.** *ser* (looks), *tener* (features), *estar* (feelings); six emotions with colours; *con mis… puedo…* for powers; imaginative verbs.
- **Ideas.** Superhero builder that reads the description back; power invention writing prompt; emotion colour wheel.

## Recap (end of unit)
Five boxes: advice, routine sentence order, body parts, s / x words, plus a final fill-in about a hidden object. Ours: `fill-gap-choice` (advice), `order-words` (routines), `spell-tiles` (body), `spell-fill-letters` (s/x), `type-the-answer`, plus `listen-pick-word`.

## Difficulty progression
S1 name and describe → S3 advise and use reflexives → S5 sequence and retell → S7 describe imaginatively. New archetypes needed: `order-words`, `verb-conjugator` (reflexives), `syllable-order`, `crossword-lite`, `who-is-who`, `veo-veo`.
