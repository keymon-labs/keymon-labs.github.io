# Unit 1 working notes (paraphrased)

Greetings and family, paper sessions 1, 3, 5, 7, plus the end-of-unit recap. Own words only: story beats are short and generic; names of new characters are for us to invent. Archetype ids: [ARCHETYPES.md](../ARCHETYPES.md). Unit sketches: [UNITS.md](../UNITS.md). Topics and rules summary: [BOOK-NOTES.md](../BOOK-NOTES.md).

## S1 Introductions
- **Story beats.** No comic. A warm-up word list, then a short chat where friends meet and give names and ages, and one introduces the teacher. A greeting song asks after each family member (not reproduced; write our own).
- **Exercise kinds.** Own word list (`free-list`, new) · odd one out in word clouds (`odd-one-out`) · listen, mark five statements true/false (`true-false-listen`) · write names from a picture (`type-the-answer`, new) · read a dialogue and say it aloud (`dialogue-complete`, `say-it` new) · underline family words in a text (`tap-in-text`, new) · numbers 0-10, digit ↔ word (`spell-tiles`, `match-pairs`) · colour by number code (`colour-by-code`) · whisper-chain and question-ball classroom games (`whisper-chain`, new) · recall without the book (`type-the-answer`) · classmate survey (`survey-tally`, new) · exit riddle with an everyday answer (`veo-veo`). Drawing yourself is off-app.
- **Language.** Numbers 0-10; about six colours; about ten family words; about five pets; about four greetings. Self-introduction with *llamarse*; age with *tener*; first and third person of the same verb. No new spelling rule.
- **Ideas.** Greeting-by-time-of-day tap game; cast intro screen (one original line each); original greeting song for the title screen.

## S3 Days and ñ
- **Story beats.** A comic. A boy video-calls a cousin, learns the next day is his grandfather's name day, and must find a surprise present. Shopping trip, a choice between gifts and colours, a family party, and an unsure ending about the colour. New side characters appear (shop assistant, relatives): ours will be renamed.
- **Exercise kinds.** Read comic, act out (off-app) · comprehension, three choices plus one true/false (`read-answer`) · listen and match to days (`listen-pick-word`) · unscramble days (`spell-tiles`) · sort masculine/feminine (`sort-into-bins`) · un/una/unos/unas (`fill-gap-choice`) · singular to plural (`spell-fill-letters`, focus plural) · write names with capitals (`type-the-answer`) · alphabet letter names and missing letters (`match-pairs`, `spell-fill-letters`) · complete words with ñ (`spell-fill-letters`) · match famous writers/painters to professions (`match-pairs`) · exit code from numbers to letters (`code-breaker`). Drawing and decorating are off-app.
- **Language.** Seven days, lowercase; about ten everyday objects; about five animals; alphabet letter names including ñ; about a dozen ñ words. Articles with gender and number, a few nouns with unexpected endings; plural -s / -es; capitals on names; ñ is its own letter. Our own ñ examples: otoño, sueño, dueño, mañana.
- **Ideas.** Name-day culture card; famous-names match with a fact reveal; shopping mini-game (present within a budget, article agreement feedback); daily "what day is it" prompt.

## S5 Family and ser / estar / tener
- **Story beats.** A short first-person text, not a comic. A big, lively family gathers weekly: card games, reading, singing over video calls, and a mischievous small pet. Ends by asking the reader about their own family.
- **Exercise kinds.** Circle correct answers, match characters to facts (`read-answer`, `match-pairs`) · circle ¿ ¡ in the text, copy examples (`punctuation-pick`, `type-the-answer`) · read questions aloud (`say-it`) · complete a ser/estar/tener table and conjugate in sentences (`verb-conjugator`) · underline verbs (`tap-in-text`) · verbs in a snake-shaped grid and family word search (`word-search`) · match pictures to sentences (`match-pairs`) · listen and complete with the right verb (`fill-gap-choice` with audio) · split a run-on sentence (`spot-the-words`) · flags of programme countries (`match-pairs`, `memory-flip`, `colour-by-code`). The book has no spelling item here; we add a missing-vowels one (`spell-fill-letters`).
- **Language.** Extra family words (grandmother, cousin, aunt); about five pets; about six feelings; about a dozen countries and five languages; about five hobbies. Present of *ser* (identity), *estar* (state, feelings, place), *tener* (possession, age) with yo / tú / él-ella. Question words: qué, cómo, de dónde, en qué país, cuándo. ¿ ¡ at start and end; exclamations show surprise, joy or anger.
- **Ideas.** Flag and country memory game (our own flag art); family card game ("do you have..."); mini-interview of an invented family; reorder a comic strip of a Sunday routine.

## S7 My passport
- **Story beats.** No comic. Passport-style pages for several people (names, languages, birthdays), then a picture riddle at a family gathering with clues about clothes and feelings, a family bingo and a "what is happening" picture.
- **Exercise kinds.** Read passports, note languages (`match-pairs`) · questions about a passport (`read-answer`) · fill own passport (`form-card`, new) · five clues identify a character (`who-is-who`, clue version) · talk about a picture (`say-it`) · add ¿ or ¡ (`punctuation-pick`) · listen and circle colours or places (`listen-pick-word`) · colour the verbs in a word cloud (`sort-into-bins`) · make ñ words from a letter grid (`spell-tiles`, `extraLetters`) · family bingo (`bingo`) · interview classmates (`survey-tally`; the microphone craft is off-app).
- **Language.** Personal data fields (name, surnames, birthday, birthplace, country, mother tongue, languages learnt and wanted); about five languages; about four professions; about six colours. Third-person present; plural question *cuáles son*; accents on question words; ñ and ¿ ¡ inside sentences.
- **Ideas.** Card on the co-official languages of Spain; passport stamp reward per finished unit; bingo with a free centre square.

## Recap (end of unit)
A self-check page with five boxes and one coded line. Ours can mirror it: articles (`fill-gap-choice`), ¿ ¡ (`punctuation-pick`), colour names (`spell-tiles`), ñ words (`spell-fill-letters`), family and a number (`type-the-answer`), and a decoded phrase (`code-breaker`, our own phrase). Add one `listen-pick-word` item so the mix rule is met.

## Caveats
Exercise contents of puzzles (word search, snake grid, letter tiles) are recorded by kind only. The recap's coded line and puzzle answers are deliberately not noted. Three page layouts were spot-checked against images; the rest comes from the page text.
