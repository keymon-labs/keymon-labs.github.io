# Unit 2 working notes (paraphrased)

School life, paper sessions 1, 3, 5, 7, plus the end-of-unit recap. Own words only: story beats are short and generic; names of new characters are for us to invent. Archetype ids: [ARCHETYPES.md](../ARCHETYPES.md). Unit sketches: [UNITS.md](../UNITS.md). Same format as [u1.md](u1.md).

## S1 Friends at school
- **Story beats.** No comic. A warm-up word list, then a short chat where friends say which school subject or activity they like or dislike. A song about question words (where, how much, how, what) is not reproduced; write our own.
- **Exercise kinds.** Own word list (`free-list`) · odd one out in clouds of school words (`odd-one-out`) · listen, mark five statements true/false about a class scene (`true-false-listen`) · write four things from your pencil case (`type-the-answer`) · read a dialogue about likes and say it aloud (`dialogue-complete`, `say-it`) · underline the question words in a song (`tap-in-text`) · put el / la in front of objects and sort nouns into four columns: el, la, los, las (`sort-into-bins`) · answer "do you like...?" with mucho / bastante / un poco / nada (`fill-gap-choice`) · twin-word board game, match each word card to its pair (`memory-flip`, `match-pairs`) · sort objects by gender (`sort-into-bins`) · backpack survey (`survey-tally`) · exit riddle about a classroom object (`veo-veo`).
- **Language.** About 30 school nouns (classroom, objects, places in the school, people); four degrees of liking; *me gusta* + infinitive; four question words; el/la/los/las and plural -s.
- **Ideas.** "Pack your bag" drag game (pick the right objects for a lesson); likes slider game with a mascot reacting; original question-word song.

## S3 A week at school
- **Story hooks.** A comic that follows a week: playground games, lunch in the canteen, something forgotten in school, a clock in the dining room. It carries days, hours and positions of objects.
- **Exercise kinds.** Read comic, act out (off-app) · comprehension with three choices (`read-answer`) · find objects in the comic scene by position (`place-the-object`, `tap-in-text`) · read analogue clocks and write the time (`clock-reader`) · match people to places and times in a maze (`match-pairs`) · position words *encima / debajo / delante / detrás* (`place-the-object`, `fill-gap-choice`) · listen, complete and match to a picture (`listen-pick-word`) · *del / de la* (`fill-gap-choice`) · the r / rr rule (`spell-fill-letters` focus r/rr) · write four words of each kind (`type-the-answer`) · culture page on traditional playground games, match game to description (`match-pairs`, `read-answer`) · exit code from numbers to letters (`code-breaker`).
- **Language.** Telling the time: *es la una*, *son las…*, *en punto*, *y media*, *y cuarto*, *menos cuarto*; four position words; *de + el = del*; school meals and subjects; the r rule: one r at word start or after n, l, s sounds strong, rr between vowels, single r between vowels is soft.
- **Ideas.** Clock-setting drag game (turn the hands to a heard time); timetable builder; a "what is hidden" scene where objects must be placed from spoken instructions.

## S5 Magic words
- **Story hooks.** A short story set in a classroom where wall posters with polite phrases start to talk, but only to children who look and use good manners. Models polite requests and classroom language and colours of classroom things.
- **Exercise kinds.** Match sentence halves from the story (`match-pairs`, `read-answer`) · describe your own classroom with colours and counts (`fill-gap-choice`, `type-the-answer`) · read short phrases aloud (`say-it`) · conjugate regular present verbs: -ar, -er, -ir, yo / tú / él-ella (`verb-conjugator`) · underline verbs in sentences, find verbs in a snake grid (`tap-in-text`, `word-search`) · match pictures to sentences (`match-pairs`) · fill sentences from a verb bank (`fill-gap-choice`) · replace a name with a verb (`fill-gap-choice` verb form) · memory game: look, cover, write what you remember (`memory-flip`, `type-the-answer`) · split a run-on sentence (`spot-the-words`) · add the time to routine sentences (`fill-gap-choice`, `type-the-answer`) · word search with exit letters (`word-search`).
- **Language.** Polite phrases (please, thank you, can I, can you, how do you say); about eight regular verbs in the present with three persons; about six colours and room parts; a few connector time words.
- **Ideas.** Talking-posters game (tap the poster that fits the situation); verb-ending conveyor belt; a politeness-streak reward that rewards kind phrases.

## S7 Off to class
- **Story beats.** No comic. A phrase bank for asking and thanking in class, short answer practice, a picture with objects whose positions are judged true/false, drawing from instructions, and a timetable to fill by listening.
- **Exercise kinds.** Complete polite phrases (`fill-gap-choice`, `type-the-answer`) · match requests to replies (`dialogue-complete`) · true/false on a picture by position (`true-false-listen`, `place-the-object`) · read instructions and arrange objects (`place-the-object`) · listen and fill a weekly timetable (`listen-pick-word`, `clock-reader`) · colour the verbs in a cloud (`sort-into-bins`) · bingo with six classroom objects (`bingo`) · talk about a picture (`say-it`). Crown craft is off-app.
- **Language.** About ten request and reply phrases; clock times with quarters and half hours; positions; days; verbs from S5.
- **Ideas.** Classroom politeness role play with the mascot; "build the timetable" puzzle; bingo with a free centre square.

## Recap (end of unit)
Five boxes: likes, telling time, classroom nouns, r / rr, plus a final riddle with an object answer. Ours: `fill-gap-choice` (me gusta), `clock-reader`, `spell-tiles` (objects), `spell-fill-letters` (r/rr), `type-the-answer`, plus one `listen-pick-word`.

## Difficulty progression
S1 recognise and sort words → S3 read time and place things → S5 conjugate and write sentences → S7 combine requests, time and position. New archetypes needed: `clock-reader`, `place-the-object`, `sort-into-bins`, `verb-conjugator`.
