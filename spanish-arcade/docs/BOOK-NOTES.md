# Book notes: curriculum map

Digest of *Mis unidades de español. Primer cuatrimestre A1, Serie Luna*, the course book the app is inspired by. Read from the book so other chats don't have to.

**Cast.** The app follows the book's stories and features its cast, drawn in our own original style, with our own lines. One line each: **Luz**, the Spanish teacher; **Gus** (kind boy, neighbour of Gas, hamster Bigotes) and **Gas** (lively, clever girl with a colourful braid, cat Alba) are the two leads; **Toni**, the elk mascot of the programme; **Diego** (loves farm animals, speaks Spanish and French, rabbit Jito); **Raúl** (Rita's best friend, tells funny jokes, dog Robi); **Rita** (tallest, loves sport, chameleon Molón); **Beatriz** (good at maths, loves reading, fish) and her older sister **Ainara** (loves singing and dancing, parrot Lolo). Use them for the guide role, hints, jokes and unit intros; keep them in character.

**What is recorded here:** topics, grammar points, spelling rules and the *kinds* of exercise (facts and ideas), with a few common example words per topic. **What is not:** the book's own word lists and exercise contents, stories, comics, songs, riddles, dialogues, illustrations, audio or activity names. Write original lists and sentences on the same topics. The book and the full page text are kept privately by the owner (private assets repo, folder `book/`); ask the owner for access. They are never committed here (`*.pdf` is git-ignored).

Level: A1. Three units × four paper sessions (1, 3, 5, 7; the even sessions are online and not in the PDF). Each game unit in this app maps to one paper session, so 12 game units cover the topics.

## Session rhythm in the book (useful for unit shape)
Session 1: word list, odd-one-out, listening true/false, dialogue practice, vocabulary/grammar basics. Session 3: comic + comprehension questions, grammar and spelling rule, writing practice. Session 5: short story + comprehension, verbs, speaking. Session 7: writing practice, clue-in-the-picture, bingo. Each unit ends with a recap page (completing, ordering, a coded word, a word search) and a culture page.

## Unit 1. Hola, ¿cómo estás?
| Session | Theme | Vocabulary | Language / spelling |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Introductions | numbers 0–10; basic colours; close family (padre, madre, abuelo/a, hermano/a…); sorting words into categories | *Yo me llamo… / Ella se llama… / ¿Cuántos años tienes? Yo tengo … años.* Greetings (*hola, ¿qué tal?, buenos días, adiós*) |
| S3 | A party and days | days of the week (lunes…domingo, lowercase); everyday objects and animals | *un/una/unos/unas*, singular/plural (-s, -es); capitals for names; the Spanish alphabet and letter names; **ñ** (a dozen common ñ words: e.g. España, niño, montaña) |
| S5 | My family | family words, pets, feelings, places, flags/countries (a few countries and languages) | **¿? ¡!** at start and end; present of **ser / estar / tener** (soy, eres, es; estoy, estás, está; tengo, tienes, tiene); questions ¿Cómo estás? ¿Cómo te llamas? ¿De dónde eres? ¿En qué país vives? ¿Cuándo es tu cumpleaños? |
| S7 | My passport | personal data (nombre, apellidos, cumpleaños, lugar de nacimiento, país, lengua materna), languages, famous surnames/professions (escritor/a, pintor/a) | describing a person from clues; ¿? ¡! practice; recap of ñ, days, un/una |

## Unit 2. La hora de Español
| Session | Theme | Vocabulary | Language / spelling |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | School friends | classroom objects (≈20, e.g. bolígrafo, libro, mochila); school places (clase, patio, biblioteca…); school people | **el / la / los / las**; *me gusta / no me gusta* + *mucho, bastante, un poco, nada*; question words dónde, cuánto, cómo, qué |
| S3 | A week at school | clock times, mealtimes, school subjects, objects in a scene | the time: *es la una en punto, son las dos y media, y cuarto, menos cuarto*; position: **encima, debajo, delante, detrás (de)**; **del / de la**; **r vs rr** (soft r; strong r at word start, rr between vowels, r after n/l/s) |
| S5 | Magic words | classroom phrases, politeness | present of regular verbs **-ar / -er / -ir** (estudiar, leer, escribir, cocinar, comer, jugar): yo -o, tú -as/-es, él/ella -a/-e; colours and description of the classroom |
| S7 | Off to class | polite expressions: por favor, perdón, gracias, de nada, lo siento, ¿puedo ir al baño?, ¿puedes hablar más despacio?, ¿cómo se dice…? | answering polite requests; describing positions in a picture; reading a timetable; recap of r/rr |
Culture: traditional Spanish playground games.

## Unit 3. Dime qué ves
| Session | Theme | Vocabulary | Language / spelling |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | The senses | the five senses and the verbs for them; body parts (≈15); basic feelings (contento, triste, enfadado…) | *Estoy + muy / bastante / un poco + feeling*; describing with **ser / tener**: pelo moreno, rubio, pelirrojo, castaño, corto, largo, rizado, liso; ojos azules, verdes, marrones, grandes, pequeños |

## Unit 1 detail (digest)
Per-session notes in our own words: [book/u1.md](courses/a1s1/u1.md). What the table above leaves out:
- **S1** has no comic: a word list, a short meeting chat, a greeting song, then games (odd one out, colour-by-number, classmate survey, whisper chain). About ten family words, about five pets, colours and numbers 0-10.
- **S3** is the unit's comic (story hooks and exercises in [book/u1.md](courses/a1s1/u1.md)). Alphabet letter names (with ñ) and capitals on names are taught here too.
- **S5** is a short text, not a comic. The book has no spelling item in this session; the flags (culture) page sits here, not in S7.
- **S7** is built on passport-style pages (personal data, languages, professions), a picture riddle, family bingo and a "what is happening" picture. Accents on question words appear.
- **Recap** page: five self-check boxes (articles, ¿ ¡, colours, ñ, family and numbers) and one coded line.
- **Speaking, free writing and drawing** appear in every session. Most have no archetype yet: see the new ideas (`type-the-answer`, `say-it`, `tap-in-text`, `form-card`, `survey-tally`, `whisper-chain`, `free-list`) in [ARCHETYPES.md](ARCHETYPES.md).

## Kinds of exercise seen in the book → archetype ideas
Odd one out · listen and mark true/false · listen and match to pictures · listen and complete · read and answer a/b/c · read and mark V/F · match with arrows · classify into el/la or un/una · complete tables (verbs, articles) · fill missing letters (ñ, r/rr, s/x, vowels) · unscramble letters · put words in order to make a sentence · put syllables in order · separate a run-on sentence · add ¿? or ¡! · read a clock and write the time · place objects by position words · find verbs in a letter snake or word search · coded word (number→letter) · crossword · bingo · memory game · who-is-who (yes/no questions) · veo-veo. ## Notes for content writers
- The audience already speaks Spanish; the challenge is **written** Spanish (spelling, accents, punctuation, grammar) and school-style vocabulary, not understanding speech. Make audio easy and spelling the focus.
- Keep sentences short; use present tense; picture or emoji support for every word.
- Don't copy the book's wording for sentences or stories; invent our own, on the same topics.
