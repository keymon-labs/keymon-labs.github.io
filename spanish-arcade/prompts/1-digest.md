# 1. Digest (private chat, with your book attached)

```text
I will give you a Spanish course book for children. Write a curriculum map in your own words that I can publish
in a public repository. Use this structure: course shape (level, term, audience, number of units and sections);
one table per book unit (section, theme, vocabulary as CATEGORIES with a count and 2-3 example words,
grammar, spelling rule); the rhythm of a section as KINDS of exercise; culture topics; notes for writers.

Hard rules:
- Record only facts: topics, grammar points, spelling rules, level, counts, kinds of exercise.
- Do not reproduce stories, comics, dialogues, songs, poems, riddles, jokes, exercise items, answer keys,
  puzzle grids, activity titles, page numbers, the book's word lists, or the characters' names and looks.
- Everyday words and phrases of up to 5 words are fine (me gusta). No quote longer than 8 words.
- Do not name the book, the publisher or the authors.
- Invent nothing about real people. No personal data of any kind.
Before answering, delete every sentence that is more than 5 words of the book's own wording.
```
