Voice Pad

Slide a finger on the pad and Gloop sings. Left to right changes the note. Top to bottom changes the vowel.

Theory: what you are hearing

Voice types

    Registers

      Vowels and formants

      Every vowel is the same buzz shaped by the mouth. The mouth boosts three bands of sound called formants. Moving down the pad changes where those bands sit, so oo turns into ah and then ee.

        Vibrato

        Hold still and Gloop wobbles the note a little, like a trained singer. Swipe fast and the wobble goes away.
