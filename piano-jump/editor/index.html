Tips for a good scan

  • One staff at a time. You will crop to a single line of music, starting just after the clef and time signature.
  • Keep it clean. Crop out lyrics, pencil marks, finger numbers, titles and pictures. They confuse the reader.
  • Two staves joined by a brace (grand staff)? Scan the top line, then the bottom line, and pick the matching clef.
  • Flat, bright, straight. Hold the page flat in good light. If the lines slope, use Straighten.
  • It is a first draft. Rests, sharps and ties are not read. You will check and fix everything in the editor.
