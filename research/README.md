## How to add an entry
One row per thing in the note it belongs to, or a new note when it is a new theme. Each entry says:
- **Link** to the live site and to the repo, if any
- **Licence** as read from the repo or terms, and whether it was checked or only seen in a snippet
- **Free?** and **type** (game, tool, tutorial)
- **Tech** behind it (library names)
- **Learn / try** what we might take from it, as an idea

## Rules for reuse
- A note here is not permission to copy. Before any third-party code, asset or sound goes into an app, check its licence and add a [CREDITS.md](../CREDITS.md) row in the same commit.
- Compatible without asking: public domain, CC0, MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, ISC, OFL, CC BY, CC BY-SA. Ask first for GPL family, CC BY-NC or ND, "free for personal use", or no stated licence.
- Ideas, mechanics and genres are free to reuse. Copy the idea, not the art, names, tracks or level designs.
- Proprietary sites are analysed from the outside only. Their terms usually ban copying and mirroring, so no backups or copies of their code or assets in this repo.
