# Pentatonic sequencer (exploring)

Explicit interest to explore. Reference points: ToneMatrix Redux (click grid, particles, share link), Pocket Sequencer (rows are scale notes), Shape Your Music (drawing instead of a grid), aQWERTYon (no wrong notes).

## Questions to explore (suggested, not decided)
- Input: touch grid, camera hand position, shapes, or the real piano.
- Variations: surprise-me button (procedural), shareable song link, MIDI export, scale choice.
- Tone.js (MIT) is the natural base; do not copy the GPL repos.
