# Music theory learning sites

- **musictheory.net**: free web lessons and exercises, paid iOS apps; no account needed.
- **Musicca**: free exercises for notes, rhythm, intervals, chords, scales; tools like metronome, circle of fifths and chord finder; classroom mode; free account removes ads.
- **muted.io**: free interactive scale, chord, rhythm and drum-pattern references.
- **MyMusicTheory**: free exercises with progress tracking.
