# Babylon.js

Mentioned in Rosebud's blog posts for 3D. More batteries than Three.js, larger download (over 1 MB).

## Capabilities
- WebGL and an early, complete WebGPU backend; Havok physics built in; native WebXR; Playground (online editor) and Inspector (in-browser scene editor); Babylon Native for desktop and mobile.
