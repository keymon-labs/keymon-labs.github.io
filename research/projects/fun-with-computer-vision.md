# Fun With Computer Vision

By the [collidingScopes](../authors/collidingscopes.md) creator. The site says 40 tutorials with project files and written walkthroughs for a one-off $10 lifetime access, and 500+ learners. Camera demos run in the browser, best on a laptop.

## Demos worth a look (from the site's list)
Hand Tracking 101, Shape Creator, Planet Explorer (control a 3D globe), Whirlpool Camera (wave to distort your webcam), Seefood (hot dog or not, a hand-tracking game), Chin-up Game, Laser Kitten Defense (eye lasers), Marble Aquarium, Voxel Editor, Watercolor (shaders by hand movement), Wingman (flap your arms to fly, a workout game), Guitar Hero (play chords for real).

## Learn
- The range of ideas: each demo is one gesture mapped to one effect.
- Wingman and Chin-up Game are exercise games, close to the movement-games theme.
