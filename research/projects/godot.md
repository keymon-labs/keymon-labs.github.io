# Godot

Full engine rather than a library. Exports to Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android and the web. The web export is heavy (about 15 to 30 MB) next to a Phaser build under 1 MB.

## Capabilities
- 2D and 3D, node and scene tree, built-in editor, physics, animation.
- Text-based scene and script files and a command-line launcher make it unusually easy for coding agents to drive without opening the editor.

## Reuse
MIT: the engine and exported games are free to use. Not a fit for our plain-JS-on-Pages shape, so ideas only for now.
