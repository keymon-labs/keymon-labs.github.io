# Pocket Sequencer (ArcadeLab)

Each row is a note from a pentatonic scale and each column a beat; tap cells to build a loop and a playhead triggers them through Web Audio. Its FAQ gives the reason it works for beginners: a five-note scale with no harsh intervals, so anything tapped sounds consonant. Seen only through its listing page.
