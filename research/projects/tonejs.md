# Tone.js

MIT, about 14.9k stars. A Transport is the master clock (start, stop, loop; `Tone.Loop` repeats callbacks on it). Synths: Synth, FMSynth, AMSynth, NoiseSynth, PolySynth, plus Sampler. Effects (distortion, filter, feedback delay...) chain in series or parallel.
