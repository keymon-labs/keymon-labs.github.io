# PlayCanvas

The engine is MIT. Its collaborative web editor is a hosted product whose plans and terms were not read.

## Capabilities
- WebGPU and WebGL2, glTF, WebXR, 3D Gaussian splatting tools, a model viewer and React and web-component wrappers. One reviewer calls it the best browser-first 3D choice with the smallest runtime.
