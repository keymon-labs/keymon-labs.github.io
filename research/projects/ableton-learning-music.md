# Ableton Learning Music

Launched 2017 as a companion to Ableton's Making Music book; later translated into about 19 languages. Lessons play a pattern, then let you compare variations on the same notes in a grid (for example the Beats chapter lesson "What are these sounds?" on kick, clap, snare and hi-hats). You can make music with the mouse or computer keyboard, and export a creation as an Ableton Live Set. The same team made Learning Synths (2019) with a playground that visualises how synth parameters interact.

## Learn
- Teach one idea per screen with sound first, then name it.
- Real song case studies sit beside the toys.
- No licence or terms are stated on the lesson page; only Ableton's privacy policy and imprint are linked.
