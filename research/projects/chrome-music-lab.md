# Chrome Music Lab

Launched March 2016 by Google Creative Lab for Music in Our Schools Month; hands-on experiments on sound, rhythm and melody that work on phones, tablets and laptops. Song Maker (2018) was built with Yotam Mann and Use All Five. Experiments use Web Audio, WebMIDI, WebRTC (microphone) and Tone.js, each with a help button linking to its source. Teachers use it across music, science, maths and art.

## Learn
- Gold standard for simple, instant, no-sign-up music toys; many ideas map to our kids' music apps.
- The site blocked my fetch, so individual experiments were not reviewed; Apache-2.0 comes from a catalogue entry for the repo, not the licence file.
