# NYU Music Experience Design Lab tools

aQWERTYon maps notes to the computer keyboard so rows play scales and columns play chords: no wrong notes, which encourages free exploration. The Theory version shows notes in colours (blue darker, purple neutral, grey outside the scale) and joins played notes with lines and shapes. The lab also built Groove Pizza (listed in the Tone.js demos) and writes about designing accessible tools in the browser. Ableton's Learning Music site is said to resemble their work.
