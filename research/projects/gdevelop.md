# GDevelop

Visual events instead of code. The core, HTML5 engine, IDE and extensions are MIT; the GDevelop name and logo are not. Exported games can be sold without opening their source. Paid plans add AI credits.

## Capabilities
- Visual event logic, 2D with growing 3D, multiplayer, export to HTML5, Android, iOS and desktop. Does not generate art.

## Reuse
MIT code. Do not reuse the name or logo.
