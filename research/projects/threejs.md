# Three.js

A rendering library, not a game engine: scene, camera, materials, loaders, WebXR, audio. Small core (under about 200 kB gzipped).

## Capabilities
- WebGL, WebGL2 and WebGPU rendering; glTF and other loaders through add-ons; post-processing through add-ons.
- No built-in physics: pair it with cannon-es or Rapier.
- React Three Fiber wraps it for React apps.
- The 3D base of Rosebud and of the collidingScopes camera toys.
