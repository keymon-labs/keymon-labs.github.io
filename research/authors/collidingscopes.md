# collidingScopes (Alan)

Goes by Alan in the READMEs. Three GitHub accounts appear to be the same person: **collidingScopes** (most projects), **getToTheChopin** (the portfolio and older tools) and **stereoDrift** (the music visualiser). The evidence: the portfolio lists Stereo Drift and Colliding Scopes, the collidingScopes profile links to the portfolio, and READMEs share one Instagram handle. A large, inspiring body of small games and experiments, many open source, with paid tutorials on the side ([Fun With Computer Vision](../projects/fun-with-computer-vision.md)).

## Where to look
- [Portfolio](../projects/gettothechopin-portfolio.md): the full project list, including the games.
- [Stereo Drift](../projects/stereodrift.md): music visualiser (CC BY-NC, ask first).
- Checked and reusable (MIT): [Fruit Ninja](../projects/collidingscopes-fruit-ninja.md), [Arpeggiator](../projects/collidingscopes-arpeggiator.md), and the hand-tracking starter `threejs-handtracking-101` (MIT per its README).
- Not opened, licence unchecked: 3D Model Playground, Threejs Shape Creator, Particular Drift, Video-to-ASCII, Iron Interface (particles steered by hands and voice), Acrobatic Arcade and the other portfolio items.

## Learn
- Start with `threejs-handtracking-101` to get a camera plus landmarks running fast.
- The pattern across projects: one gesture, one effect, one page, plain JS with MediaPipe and Three.js.

## Reuse
Per project: check each licence before copying. Two accounts hold MIT code (collidingScopes); the Stereo Drift account does not.
