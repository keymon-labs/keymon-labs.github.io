# Learning music through visualisation

- Change one thing, hear and see the result at once (slider, drag, draw).
- No account, no install, works on phone and tablet.
- Show sound as blocks, colour and shape instead of staff notation, so beginners can create at once.
- Pair every toy with one concept (rhythm, waves, harmonics) and give teachers lesson ideas.
- Chrome Music Lab was built with WebGL, Pixi.js, Tone.js and WebRTC (microphone).
