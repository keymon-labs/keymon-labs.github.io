# Camera and movement games

- The body is the controller: no gamepad, so young kids can play without learning buttons.
- Browser games run tracking on the device and say so: video is never uploaded. Every site advertises this.
- Fallbacks keep games playable: touch or mouse when the camera is missing or refused.
- Hoppo's design is worth studying: short games, three input modes, a no-camera class mode, a parent view, difficulty and performance settings.
- Hardware equivalents: camera consoles on the TV (Nex Playground), Just Dance with a phone camera, Osmo (tablet plus physical pieces).

## Learn
- Gesture vocabulary for young kids should be few and forgiving: wave, pop, reach, jump.
