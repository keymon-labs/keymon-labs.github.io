# Research workspace: teaching music to kids 4–10 through games

Entry point for any session working here. Public repo: generic content only, no personal data (root CLAUDE.md rules apply).

## Goal
Build a researched foundation for music games on this site (Piano Jump, Finger Moles, Brick Beats, Beat Tiles and future ones): how to teach real music skills (correct theory, posture, finger movement, rhythm, pitch) to kids aged 4–10 who won't practise alone, by turning correct practice into play — on the real instrument, with physical objects, or through theory games.

## Read in this order
1. `INDEX.md` — every file, one line each. Start here, open only what you need.
2. `cross/synthesis.md` — the combined answer (once phase 3 is done).
3. `topics/<id>/summary.md` — per-topic digest. `notes-p1.md` / `notes-p2.md` hold the source cards behind it.
4. `pipeline.md` — how the research was run and how to extend it.
5. `backlog.md` — phase status and open questions.

## Extending
- New question on an existing topic: add it to that topic's `brief.md` under "Open", research with the card format in `pipeline.md`, append cards to a new `notes-pN.md`, then update `summary.md` and `INDEX.md`.
- New topic: copy a `topics/<id>/` folder shape, add a row in `INDEX.md` and `backlog.md`.
- Keep raw page text out. Cards only, quotes under 15 words, always the URL.

## Rules
- Facts carry a source URL. Opinion and our own design ideas are marked as such.
- Music theory and technique claims must match mainstream pedagogy; when sources disagree, record both.
- Don't copy copyrighted method-book content (exercises, songs, illustrations). Describe the idea, cite the source.
