# 06 Breaking the fourth wall and playing with the real world

Scope: games that leave the screen: real instruments, physical objects, the room, other people.

## Subquestions
- Q1 Fourth-wall breaking in games: meta techniques (addressing the player, using device features, Undertale, Doki Doki, Metal Gear's Psycho Mantis, Inscryption, Pony Island) and what made them memorable; kid-appropriate versions.
- Q2 Tangible interfaces for kids: Osmo, Nintendo Labo, toys-to-life (Skylanders), LEGO-based digital play (LEGO Super Mario, LEGO Hidden Side, Lego music experiments), Marbotic, reacTable, Bee-Bot — research on tangible learning.
- Q3 Real-instrument input: MIDI, microphone pitch/onset detection, camera hand/finger tracking — reliability and design implications for kids.
- Q4 Mixed reality / AR / projection play, camera marker tracking (ArUco/AprilTag), physical-to-digital "scan your creation" mechanics.
- Q5 Games that make kids move, sing, clap, or play with a parent in the room; "the game asks you to do something off-screen".
- Q6 Unplugged / paper / board-game hybrids for music (rhythm cards, colour-coded bells, boomwhackers) and how a screen can orchestrate them.
