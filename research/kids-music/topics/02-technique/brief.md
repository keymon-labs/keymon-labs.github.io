# 02 Technique and practice: piano, guitar, and how kids practise

Scope: correct physical technique on real instruments and the science of practice for young, reluctant learners.

## Subquestions
- Q1 Piano posture and hand shape for small children: bench height, wrist, curved fingers, finger numbers, thumb, relaxed arm weight; common faults and how teachers correct them.
- Q2 Early piano progressions: five-finger positions (C, G, middle C), black-key starts, finger independence, legato/staccato, hands separate → together, first scales and chords.
- Q3 Guitar (and ukulele as a starter) for kids 4–10: size, posture, first chords, strumming, fretting-hand shape, when to start.
- Q4 Practice science: deliberate practice, short frequent sessions, spaced/interleaved practice, motor learning, slow practice, feedback on errors, chunking — adapted to young kids.
- Q5 Motivation for kids who don't want to practise: parent role (Suzuki triangle), autonomy, routines, rewards research, what experienced teachers do.
- Q6 Ear training, singing and keeping a steady tempo (metronome use with kids, internal pulse).
- Q7 Injury/strain and bad habits that games could accidentally train (flat fingers, tension, looking at hands, rushing).
