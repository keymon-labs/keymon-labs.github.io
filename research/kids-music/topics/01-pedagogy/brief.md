# 01 Pedagogy: methods, theory and learning progression (ages 4–10)

Scope: how music is taught to young children and in what order. Instrument-agnostic foundations.

## Subquestions
- Q1 Main methods (Kodály, Orff Schulwerk, Dalcroze eurhythmics, Suzuki, Gordon Music Learning Theory, Yamaha/early-childhood programmes): core ideas, sequence, what each does best for 4–10.
- Q2 "Sound before symbol": when and how to introduce notation; readiness signs by age.
- Q3 Solfège: movable vs fixed do (incl. Spanish/European fixed-do practice), hand signs (Curwen), pitch sequence (so-mi, la, do-re-mi pentatonic...).
- Q4 Rhythm teaching: steady beat → rhythm, rhythm syllables (Kodály ta/ti-ti, Gordon du-de, word/food chants), metre, rests, tempo.
- Q5 Learning progression: a typical skill ladder by age band (4–5, 6–7, 8–10): pulse, pitch matching, inner hearing, intervals, reading, chords/harmony, improvisation, composition.
- Q6 Introducing harmony and chords to kids (I–IV–V, triads, accompaniment patterns), and scales/keys.
- Q7 What theory explanations work for this age (metaphors, stories, colours) and common misconceptions to avoid.
