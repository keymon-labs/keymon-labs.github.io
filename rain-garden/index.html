Rain dance!

Hands off the piano. Wiggle your fingers like rain. Let them flop on your knees, all soft and floppy. Now float them back, thumb on middle C.

    For grown-ups

    Rain Garden keeps the right hand in five-finger C position: thumb on middle C, one finger per key (C D E F G). A piano can't see fingers, so the game never judges them. It listens to which note, when (against your child's own steadiness) and how evenly (against this keyboard's own touch). You check the posture.

    Play together (optional)

    Turn on Duet and play low C and G together (below middle C, left side of the piano) on every tick while your child echoes the cloud. Your low notes never count against them.
