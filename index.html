Small apps and games, mostly for kids and learning, built by chatting with AI (Claude). A hobby to see what AI can make, shared free: no ads, no tracking, no sign-in. Copy the ideas and code if they help you. Build your own site like this →

Apps and games

  • Homework Reader

    Photograph handwritten homework. Claude writes it out exactly as written, then lists the mistakes and a corrected version.

    Needs Claude: your Claude account, or an Anthropic API key Phone · camera · installable
    Versions: Latest

  • Maths Arcade

    Mini games where the maths does the work. Catapult: times tables load the power, adding and taking away beat the wind, fractions pick the block to hit. Pop: balls pile up in a funnel; multiply two that touch to pop them. Match: find the pair that makes a result ball.

    Phone · tablet · installable
    Versions: Latest

  • Brick Beats

    Build music with bricks. Take a photo of your brick mat and hear it play, with a line moving over the photo and fruit rhythms to learn.

    Phone · camera · installable · works offline
    Versions: Latest

  • Beat Tiles

    Learn rhythm by playing. Every card is one beat and its picture says how many sounds fit inside: plum, apple, coconut, watermelon. Cards play as a groove you can change.

    Phone · tablet · installable · works offline · microphone optional
    Versions: Latest

  • Zoom Tunnel

    Pictures inside pictures, zooming forever. Draw or photograph each picture, mark the doorway to the next one, then pinch and fly through.

    Prototype · phone · pen · touch
    Versions: Latest

  • Piano Jump

    A platform game played on a real piano over Bluetooth or USB MIDI. Play the right note to jump across the staff.

    Chrome · Android · desktop
    Versions: Latest v2 v1

  • Spanish Arcade

    Mini games to practise written Spanish for kids: listen, read, spell and match words. Inspired by «Mis unidades de español» (A1).

    Spanish practice for kids · phone · tablet · installable
    Versions: Latest

  • Word Quest

    A story game for practising writing. Write one sentence on paper, take a photo, fix the mistakes, then watch the story happen. Make your own episodes with Claude. English and Spanish.

    Needs Claude: your Claude account, or an Anthropic API key Phone · camera · installable
    Versions: Latest
